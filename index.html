Our Animals

🐘 Elephant

The largest land animal, known for intelligence and memory. Elephants are social creatures living in herds and are herbivores.

🦒 Giraffe

The tallest land animal with a distinctive long neck. Giraffes are gentle giants that feed on leaves high in trees.

🐒 Monkey

Intelligent primates known for their playful behavior. Monkeys are social animals often found in groups swinging through forests.

🦁 Lion

The king of the jungle, a powerful predator. Lions are social cats living in prides and are known for their majestic manes.

🦏 Rhino

A massive herbivore with a distinctive horn. Rhinos are solitary animals that can weigh up to 2,300 kg.

🐼 Panda

A gentle bear native to China, primarily eating bamboo. Pandas are endangered and are symbols of wildlife conservation.
